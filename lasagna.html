Lasagna Recipe

classic rich Italian-style meat lasagna with layers of pasta, beef ragù, tomato sauce, ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan.


Ingredients (serves 8)

  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 800g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

For the cheese layer

  • 450g ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Other ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 500g shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add diced onion and minced garlic, sauté until softened.
  3. Add ground beef to the skillet, cook until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, sugar, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. In a bowl, combine ricotta cheese, egg, Parmesan cheese, and parsley.
  6. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  7. In a baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce. Layer with noodles, ricotta mixture, mozzarella cheese, and more meat sauce. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, ending with meat sauce on top.
  8. Sprinkle grated Parmesan cheese on top.
  9. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes until bubbly and golden brown.
  10. Let the lasagna rest for 10-15 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
